# SellerTaxCreditEditAndTaxBucket: back-solve Annual Taxes from the credit, a taxes clone in Buy, and the tax-bucket fix in Cash Needed

Branch `claude/festive-ride-7g1y2e` from `main` (the session's designated branch stands in for a `<task_name>` branch, as the last two plans did). First implementation commit copies this plan to `tasks/todo/SellerTaxCreditEditAndTaxBucket.md`; boxes are ticked there as work lands.

## Context

Two owner asks about the BRRRR **Seller Tax Credit**, the property-tax proration on the purchase settlement (`BackEnd/BL/analyze/common/deal_math.py:157-170`: seller's share `T × (day-of-year − 1) / days-in-year`, reversed when the seller already paid the year's bill).

1. **Edit it the other way around.** Today the credit is a read-only `AutoFigure` in the Buy section (`frontend/src/components/deal/brrr/BuySection.vue:69`). After a closing the owner knows the credit on the settlement statement and wants to type *that* and have **Annual Taxes** adjusted to match. It must **not** become an input: it stays a result, with a small pencil that opens a deliberately less-easy inline edit. Next to it, a **second clone of the Annual Taxes input** (the real one lives in Rent & Holding, `RentHoldingSection.vue:71-78`) so the two are read together; both clones always show the same value (same field, pure UI).
2. **Bug: the credit leaks into Cash Needed.** `cash_to_close_buy` subtracts the credit (correct, `closing_costs_buy.py:38-39`), and `total_cash_invested` starts from `cash_to_close_buy` (`cash_out.py:22-24`), so Cash Needed is lower by the credit. In reality the buyer pays the whole year's bill in November, so the seller's share is set aside in a per-property tax bucket the day after closing: Cash Needed must subtract it at closing and add it back the day after, net zero.

**Owner decisions (asked and answered):** the bucket is **one-sided**. A positive credit (seller still owes the year's taxes) is set aside and leaves Cash Needed unchanged. When the seller already paid (December by default) the buyer reimburses them at closing, Cash to Close is higher, and that reimbursement is a real cost that stays in Cash Needed through Cash to Close. Accepted consequence, stated here so it is a decision and not a surprise: `holding_costs` (`hml_and_holding_costs.py:31`) already accrues taxes per diem from closing to refi, so a December deal carries the closing-to-Dec-31 taxes twice (reimbursement `T × 17/365` plus accrual `T × 17/360`, about $170 on $3,600).

No new stored field, column or API field: the back-solve writes the existing `annual_property_taxes`, and the bucket is a record-only engine intermediate. The liquidity timeline does not read deal figures (verified), so it is untouched.

## Design

### A. Tax bucket fix (backend engine, explain, descriptions)

- `BackEnd/BL/analyze/brrrSteps/closing_costs_buy.py:12-21, 31-51`: `BuySettlement` gains `seller_tax_credit_set_aside_in_tax_bucket: Decimal` = `max(Decimal("0"), seller_tax_credit)` right after the credit (0 without a closing date, 0 when the credit is negative). `BrrrResultsWithIntermediates` (`brrr_results_with_intermediates.py`, next to `seller_tax_credit` around `:65`, comment updated) and the orchestrator mapping (`analyzeBRRR.py:116-117`) carry it. **Not** a response field (record-only intermediates like `holding_costs` are the norm; the breakdown step carries it to the popup and PDF; `analyzeBRRRRes` and the OpenAPI shape are unchanged).
- `BackEnd/BL/analyze/brrrSteps/cash_out.py:22-24`: `total_cash_invested` gains `+ buy_settlement.seller_tax_credit_set_aside_in_tax_bucket` **immediately after `cash_to_close_buy`** (order is load-bearing: `add_sum` folds terms with plain `==` on 28-digit Decimals, `calc_breakdown.py:138-143`, so the explain term must sit in the same slot). Comment (`:19-21`): the seller's share of this year's taxes, taken off the wire at closing and put in the property's tax bucket the day after, so it never lowers Cash Needed; a negative credit is the buyer's reimbursement and stays in Cash to Close.
- `BackEnd/BL/analyze/explain/brrr.py`:
  - After the Seller Tax Credit step (`:204-205`), a new `breakdown.add([CASH_NEEDED, "cash_out"], "Seller Tax Credit set aside in the tax bucket", results.seller_tax_credit_set_aside_in_tax_bucket, formula, note=…)` guarded by `check(results.seller_tax_credit_set_aside_in_tax_bucket == max(Decimal("0"), results.seller_tax_credit), "seller tax credit set aside")`, with two formula texts: positive → "credit received at closing, put in the property's tax bucket the day after: $X"; otherwise → "$0: a negative credit is the buyer's reimbursement to the seller and stays in Cash to Close". Filing it under the same keys as the Total Cash Invested sum matters: `max()` returns the credit's own Decimal object when positive, so two record fields share one object and `CalcBreakdown` only links such a term inside a section its step was filed under (`calc_breakdown.py:80-88, 118-119`); the existing `cash_to_close["Seller Tax Credit"]` link pin (`test_explain.py:362`) still holds because that step is under `CLOSE`. Both branches are reached by the existing scenarios (`base` positive; `december_paid_we_pay_all`, `typed_overrides` negative). This also satisfies `TestEveryFieldIsExplained` (`test_explain.py:102-111`) for the new record field.
  - Total Cash Invested `add_sum` (`:302-312`): the term `("Seller Tax Credit set aside in the tax bucket", results.seller_tax_credit_set_aside_in_tax_bucket)` right after `("Cash to Close (Buy)", …)`; note extended with the bucket sentence. Cash to Close step note (`:205`) says where a positive credit goes.
- Descriptions: `BackEnd/ReqRes/common/analyze_results.py:62-65` (`total_cash_invested`, add "+ the seller tax credit set aside for the tax bill (positive credits only)") and `:80-83` (`seller_tax_credit`, add "a positive credit is set aside in the property's tax bucket the day after closing, so it lowers cash_to_close_buy but not total_cash_invested / Cash Needed; a negative one is a real cost"); `BackEnd/mcp_server.py:74-75` glossary gets the same clause (tools/list is 256 kB of the 300 kB budget).

### B. Back-solve Annual Taxes from the credit (frontend only)

- `frontend/src/utils/brrrAutoCalc.ts`:
  - `sellerTaxCreditProratedDays(closingDay, sellerAlreadyPaidCurrentYearTaxes): number` = `doy − 1` (unpaid) or `Y − doy + 1` (paid); exported and added to `BrrrAutoCalc` as `sellerTaxCreditProratedDays: number | null` (null without a closing date) for the hint and the pencil's enabled state.
  - `annualPropertyTaxesFromSellerTaxCredit(sellerTaxCreditDollars, closingDay, sellerAlreadyPaidCurrentYearTaxes): number | null`: the exact inverse of `sellerTaxCredit` (`:112-118`) on `Math.abs(sellerTaxCreditDollars)` (the effective seller-paid state supplies the sign, so typing 177.53 or −177.53 in December both mean "I reimbursed $177.53"): `|credit| × Y / proratedDays`, rounded to cents (`annual_property_taxes` is `Numeric(12,2)`; the reproduction error is under half a cent and four decimals would change on the first autosave). `null` when the prorated days are 0 (a Jan 1 closing with unpaid taxes: every tax gives credit 0, nothing to solve).
- `frontend/src/components/deal/AutoFigure.vue` gains an opt-in edit mode; a wrapper is not smaller because the value span lives inside its root, and the read view stays the exact current markup so the 15 read-only uses are untouched:
  - props `editable?: boolean` (default false), `editAriaLabel?: string`; emit `commitEditedValue(dollars: number)`.
  - When `editable`, a `UiIconButton` (`pi pi-pencil`, `data-part="edit"`, `size="sm"`, label = `editAriaLabel ?? "Edit <label>"`, the `DayDetail.vue:87-94` pattern) sits after the value. Clicking it swaps the value span for a compact `<input type="text" inputmode="decimal" class="ui-input numeric" data-part="edit-input" :aria-label="…">` seeded with `toEditableText(value)`, auto-focused and selected; Enter/blur commit through `parseMoney(text, false)` (`utils/money.ts:48`, no `k` shorthand) and emit once when a number parsed; Escape or an empty box cancels. `v-if="value != null"` stays: with a date and empty taxes the figure reads $0 with the pencil (the entry point for "type the settlement credit, taxes follow"); with no date nothing renders and there is no back-solve.
- `BuySection.vue:67-78`:
  - `<AutoFigure … label="Seller Tax Credit" … :editable="(autoCalc.sellerTaxCreditProratedDays ?? 0) > 0" edit-aria-label="Type the credit received at closing; Annual Taxes will follow" @commit-edited-value="onSellerTaxCreditEdited" />`. Hint: "seller credits the buyer for the 9 days owned · pencil: type the credit, taxes follow" (days from `sellerTaxCreditProratedDays`; on Jan 1 unpaid: "no seller days, nothing to prorate").
  - `onSellerTaxCreditEdited(creditDollars)`: derive via `annualPropertyTaxesFromSellerTaxCredit`; if `null` do nothing (the figure snaps back to the formula value); else `field.set('annual_property_taxes', derived)`. Both clones and the figure follow from the reactive deal.
  - **Annual Taxes clone** directly under the credit figure in the same column: a `MoneyInput` with `data-testid="form.field.annualPropertyTaxesBuyClone"` (mandatory: Playwright's `fieldRoot` is a strict `getByTestId('form.field.annual_property_taxes')`, `e2e/fixtures/form.ts:24`), `label="Annual Taxes"`, `note="same field as Rent & Holding"`, `:info="impactText('annual_property_taxes')"` (already mapped), bound to `field.get/set('annual_property_taxes')` exactly like `RentHoldingSection.vue:71-78`. **No** `needed-to-run-analysis` flag on the clone: it would break the pinned DOM-ordered list of needed inputs (`DealInputsForm.test.ts:254-263`) and contradict the tab dot map (`DealInputsForm.vue:190-198`), which keeps the Rent & Holding tab as the owner of that dot. `MoneyInput` re-seeds its draft on external change (`MoneyInput.vue:66-68`), so the second box updates the moment the first commits.

### C. What stays out
No new Pydantic field, DB column, migration, or API shape; the FLIP form is untouched; the liquidity timeline is untouched; no Playwright golden beyond the one the number change forces.
